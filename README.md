# Priscilla Wambui

### I build systems that make business processes work better.

I'm interested in what happens behind the scenes of a business; how information moves, where repetitive work builds up, why systems don't always communicate, and what can be done to make things run more smoothly.

That's what drew me into automation.

I enjoy taking a process, understanding how its different parts fit together, and finding ways to connect them. Sometimes that means organising data, building a workflow between applications, automating a task or exploring where AI can take some of the manual work off someone's hands.

I'm currently developing and building across workflow automation, no-code and low-code systems, integrations and AI-powered solutions.

### A little of what I build

One of my projects is a payment reconciliation system that brings together Stripe, Airtable and automation workflows to track transactions, compare payments against invoices, flag discrepancies and make reconciliation easier to review.

It's the kind of problem I enjoy working on: practical, detailed and connected to how a business actually operates.

### My approach

I don't believe automation is about adding technology to everything. It's about understanding the process first, then finding a sensible way to improve it.

This is where I document my projects, experiments, systems and the things I'm learning along the way.
