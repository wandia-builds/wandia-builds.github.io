A little about me

Curiosity meets problem-solving.

My interest in automation comes from a simple question: why should people keep doing manually what a well-designed system could help them do more efficiently?

I enjoy taking a process apart, understanding how its different pieces fit together, and exploring how technology can connect them.

My work and learning currently span workflow automation, no-code and low-code development, application integrations, data management and AI-powered systems.

I use this space to document what I build, the problems I explore and how my approach develops with every project.

How I think about automation

Start with the process, not the tool. Understand what needs to happen, identify what can be improved, then build a solution that makes sense.
