A little about me

Curious by nature. Practical in how I learn.

I’m building my skills at the intersection of no-code tools, automation and thoughtful use of AI. I enjoy taking a process that feels scattered and working out how it could become easier to follow.

What draws me to this work

Behind many business tasks is a process: information comes in, someone makes a decision, and the next action needs to happen. I like finding ways to make those handovers more organised and less dependent on manual follow-up.

How I work

I ask questions, map the steps, keep the setup understandable and test what happens when something goes wrong. I would rather build a simple workflow that makes sense than a complicated one that is difficult to maintain.

Where I’m focusing

Tools are useful. The process matters more.

These are the kinds of problems I’m learning to solve and the areas I’m actively developing.

A project in progress

Turning payment records into a clearer picture.

A portfolio build exploring how payment and invoice information can be organised for easier reconciliation and review.

Portfolio project · Learning build

Payment reconciliation workflow

I’m using Airtable to structure payment and invoice records, compare amounts and highlight items that may need attention — such as differences, pending payments or possible duplicates.

AirtableStripe test dataMake explorationWorkflow design

What this build is designed to demonstrate

Structured tables and linked records, formula-based checks, filtered review views and a dashboard concept for seeing the status of payment records at a glance.

This is an independent learning project, not a client deployment. I’m using it to practise the decisions that go into building a dependable business workflow.
