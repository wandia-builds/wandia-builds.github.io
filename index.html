A little about me

I like understanding how the pieces fit together.

My route into automation started with an interest in how work gets done: where information is entered, how it moves from one tool to another, and where a process becomes harder than it needs to be.

I enjoy breaking those processes down, working out what needs to happen at each step, and building a practical way to bring the pieces together. That might mean structuring information in Airtable, connecting apps with Make, or exploring where AI can take on a useful part of a workflow.

I’m continuing to develop my skills through hands-on projects and focused learning. This portfolio shows that work as it grows — including what I’ve built, what I’m testing and what I’m learning along the way.

Independent portfolio project

Stripe payment reconciliation

I’m building a reconciliation workspace to organise payment records and compare them with invoice amounts. The Airtable setup includes linked records, calculated fields and review views designed to make differences easier to spot.

  • Track payment amounts, fees and net received.
  • Compare payment records with invoice values.
  • Flag potential duplicates and amounts that need review.

The project is also helping me work through how a payment workflow could connect with Stripe and Make. I’m developing that automation side as part of the build.
